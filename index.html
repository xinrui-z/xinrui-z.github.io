A little about me

About

I’m Xinrui. I work at AI startups, somewhere between operations, product, and developer relations.

I spent 2020 to 2024 in college, doing research, entering competitions, and building engineering projects on the side. After graduating, I co-founded a startup, then took six months off to reset.

I like having a few things going at once: a book next to a recipe, a long walk to work through a problem, a bike ride to make an ordinary afternoon feel a little bigger.

Off the clock

I work out, cook, bake, ride my bike, and take long walks. Time alone is how I recharge.

I read mostly history, psychology, and novels. I also have a soft spot for cats, dogs, and giant pandas.
