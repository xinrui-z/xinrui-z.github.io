Pl. II · Notes

About

I’m Xinrui. I started out as an engineer, and these days I’m a technical product manager. Engineering is still my main tool, but now I point it at the product: I build tools that help product and ops teams move faster and make better calls.

I spent 2020 to 2024 at university. Along the way, I did research, took part in academic competitions, and built engineering projects, from algorithms to full-stack apps. In my final year, from November 2023 to June 2024, I did more research and an internship at an AI lab.

In July 2024, I co-founded an AI startup that built consumer apps on top of large language models, and I had a hand in everything from product strategy to coordinating the team. When that chapter wrapped up in January 2025, I took six months off to step back and recharge, and I came back with a much clearer sense of what I want to do.

I’ve been an algorithm engineer, a full-stack developer, a product manager, and once in a while a designer. I like switching hats, because each role shows me a different side of how things get built.

Outside of work

I work out, cook (mostly Chinese food, plus a lot of baking), ride my bike, and take long walks. I’m happy in my own company, and quiet time alone is how I recharge.

I love reading about history and psychology.

I love animals, especially cats and dogs, and I have a soft spot for giant pandas.
